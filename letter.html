A Letter For My Love

Dear Gofren,

I love you. I almost can't believe it's been three years since we started dating. Time has flown by, and I feel like we've grown so much together. We've had so many ups and downs, but I wouldn't trade a single moment of it. You are my best friend, my partner, and my love. I adore you. I really hope you know it and feel it. I love you so much.

Three years, and I just can't imagine ever being tired of you or of being with you. I can't imagine my life without you. I can't imagine loving anyone else the way that I love you. You are my world, and I am so lucky that we are together. You are the most amazing person anyone could ever know. You are kind, intelligent, hardworking, beautiful, and so much more. You are everything I could ever want in a partner, and I am so grateful to have you in my life. I love you more than words can express, and I can't wait to see what the future holds for us. I love you forever and always.

Love,

Your Sofia
